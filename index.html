What you need

A Windows PC

Jaris is a Windows app. You run the installer and off you go.

An NVIDIA graphics card, if possible

It's not required, but it's recommended: Jaris answers faster and can run better models.

Nothing to configure

On first launch, Jaris installs what it needs and picks the models that suit your machine by itself.

Questions people ask me

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no credits to buy. The AI runs on your computer, so it costs me nothing to keep it running for you.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your conversations, your voice and Jaris's memory stay on your PC. Jaris only goes online when you ask it to, for example for a web search.

Do I need a powerful PC?

Not necessarily. Jaris looks at your machine and picks models that suit it. With an NVIDIA graphics card, it will be faster and smarter. Without one, it still works, just more slowly.

Does it work without the Internet?

Yes, once it's installed. You need a connection to download it and for the first launch, while it fetches its models. After that, only the features that need the web (search, access from your phone) require a connection.

Who is behind Jaris?

Me, Léo. I've been building Jaris on my own for five months, because I wanted an assistant that truly belongs to me.
